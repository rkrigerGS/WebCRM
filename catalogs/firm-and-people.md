# Firm & People Catalog

This file holds everything the email engine knows about GovSpring Legal, Marcos, and
the team. It is a living document: the app appends to it as approved emails reveal new
facts, and you can edit any line by hand.

Two things to understand about how it is used:

1. The BOILERPLATE section at the bottom is inserted into emails verbatim. The engine
   never paraphrases it. Signature, phone numbers, links, and the CC line live there so
   they are always exact.

2. Everything else is reference material the engine may draw on for personalization.
   Each fact carries a confidence tag. The engine treats CONFIRMED facts as safe to state
   plainly, treats LIKELY facts as usable but softened, and never asserts UNVERIFIED
   facts as though they were established. When you confirm or correct something, change
   its tag.
